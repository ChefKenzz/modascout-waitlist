ModaScout Fit Check — privacy notice

Applies to the ModaScout Fit Check browser extension. Last updated 8 October 2026.

The short version

Your measurements live in your browser on your computer. The extension sends nothing anywhere — with one exception you have to switch on yourself: the friends test.

What the extension stores on your computer

A nickname, the body measurements you type in, and the results of fit checks you run. All of it stays in your browser's local extension storage. Removing the extension deletes it.

What the extension reads

When you press “Check this page”, it reads the page you are looking at — once, with your click — to find the size chart. It does not track your browsing, use analytics, or load anything from third parties. Reading charts from pictures happens entirely on your computer.

The friends test (optional, off by default)

If you tick “Join the friends test” and then complete an “I own this” check, this is sent to ModaScout's private test database: your nickname, your measurements, the brand and product link, the size you own, our verdict and its confidence, your answers about how the item really fits, and the date. Not sent: your email, your real name, photos, or anything else you browse.
